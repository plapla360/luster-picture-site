## 1. Handling of photo data

- **Local processing:** All photo data selected by the user is processed entirely within the user's device (offline).
- **No external transmission:** The App does not upload photo data to external servers, store it on our servers, or share it with third parties.

## 2. Advertising and identifiers

The App uses Google AdMob for advertising.

- **Information collected:** Device identifiers (including IDFA), cookies, and device information may be collected automatically to optimize ad delivery and prevent fraud.
- **Purpose:** This information may be used to display relevant ads and measure advertising performance.
- **Control:** Users can opt out of tracking at any time through iOS Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

For details about Google's data practices, please review [Google's advertising policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads?hl=en).

## 3. Usage analysis

We may collect non-personally identifiable statistical information—including OS version, device model, crash logs, and feature usage frequency—to improve service quality and fix bugs.

## 4. Disclaimer

The App uses proprietary algorithms and AI technology for image enhancement but does not guarantee the accuracy of results or suitability for a particular purpose. The developer is not liable for damages arising from use of the App.

## 5. Changes to this policy

The App complies with applicable Japanese law. We may review and update this policy as necessary without prior notice.
